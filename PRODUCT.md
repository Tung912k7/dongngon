# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Literature enthusiasts, writers, poets, and readers looking for a quiet, focused space for slow literary consumption, creative writing, and poetic expression away from the noise of algorithmic feeds.

## Product Purpose

Đồng ngôn serves as a digital sanctuary that preserves and fosters Vietnamese literature, poetry (both traditional forms like Lục Bát and modern free verse), and prose in a serene, distraction-free environment.

## Brand Personality

- 3-Word Personality: Serene, Literary, Organic
- Voice and Tone: Reflective, quiet, craft-focused, welcoming, and deeply cultural.
- Emotional Goals: Calm focus, slow contemplation, aesthetic delight, and a sense of shared belonging.

## Anti-references

- SaaS landing-page clichés (feature grid, neon accents, gradient text, high-contrast Neo-Brutalism shadows, and large 32px+ rounded corners).
- Overstimulating, rapid-fire social media feeds.
- Complex dashboards, micro-metric trackers, and noisy notification centers.

## Design Principles

- **Design for Reading (Slow Consumption):** Typography, line-length, and breathing room must prioritize readability and deep immersion in literature.
- **Asymmetric Harmony:** Use asymmetry, negative space, and organic grouping over rigid, identical grids to evoke the feel of a premium printed literary magazine.
- **Digital Zen:** The interface acts as a silent frame for the poetry. Interactions should feel deliberate, smooth (using spring transitions), and calm.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Contrast ratio ≥ 4.5:1 for body copy against the warm paper-cream background.
- Reduced motion support (`@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)`) for all Framer Motion and CSS transitions.
- Semantic HTML tags and keyboard navigation compatibility.
